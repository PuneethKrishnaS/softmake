# Business Portal Specifications

## Scope
The Client Business Portal (`business.softmake.in`) is designed to handle corporate customer relationship modules.

## Features
- **Client Billing Dashboard**: Displays payment histories and pending invoice milestones.
- **Invoices**: View billing lines, statuses (Paid, Pending, Overdue), and download invoices.
- **Legal Contracts**: Secure signoffs and tracking for Master Services Agreements (MSA) and Service Level Agreements (SLA).
- **Client CRM Pipeline**: Track active conversations and contract renewals.
- **Support Desk**: Direct messaging channel to coordinate milestones with the software team.
